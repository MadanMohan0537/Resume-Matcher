# Tailoring guide

## Evidence map

For each material job requirement, record:

| Requirement | Candidate evidence | Status | Resume action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exact match | Direct proof in source | Supported | Feature prominently |
| Related experience | Transferable but not identical | Adjacent | Describe precisely; do not overstate |
| No evidence | Nothing verified | Unsupported | Omit or list as a gap |

Prioritize repeated requirements, responsibilities near the top of the posting, and explicit minimum qualifications. Treat aspirational language and generic company copy as lower priority.

## Bullet construction

Use the strongest structure the evidence supports:

`Action + product/problem + scope + outcome`

Prefer concrete outcomes such as revenue, adoption, conversion, retention, efficiency, quality, reliability, customer reach, or time saved. Preserve approximate markers when the source uses them. If no measurable result exists, state scope and purpose without inventing a number.

Avoid duty-only openings such as “Responsible for,” vague collaboration claims, and copied job-description sentences. Accurate terminology may be mirrored, but the candidate's experience must remain in their own evidence.

## Gap questions

Ask only questions likely to improve the resume, grouped into one message. Good questions target ownership, audience or scale, before/after state, business result, dates, tools actually used, or the candidate's preferred role. Accept “unknown” and proceed without fabricating.
