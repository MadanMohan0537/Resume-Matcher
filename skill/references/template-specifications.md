# The 5 Recruiter-Grade, ATS-Guaranteed Templates

Every template adheres strictly to ATS single-column rules, standard typography, and clean text parsing across Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Taleo, and Ashby.

---

## 1. Template 01: Executive Serif (`01-executive-serif`)
- **Best For:** Senior, Lead, Staff, Principal, Director, Head of Product/Engineering, VP, CPO, and Ex-Founders.
- **Typography:** EB Garamond / Georgia (Classic, authoritative serif).
- **Header:** Centered Name (22-25pt bold), centered title, centered contact details separated by pipes (`|`).
- **Dividers:** 0.5pt full-width black line under uppercase section titles.
- **Experience Style:** Company name (left) + Location (right); Title (left) + Dates (right); Italicized 1-line company context line; Outcome bullets.

---

## 2. Template 02: Modern Minimal (`02-modern-minimal`)
- **Best For:** Mid-level PMs, Software Engineers, and modern tech company applicants (The reliable default).
- **Typography:** Inter / Helvetica / Arial (Clean modern sans-serif).
- **Header:** Left-aligned bold name with compact contact strip.
- **Dividers:** Clean, subtle horizontal dividers (0.5pt slate).
- **Experience Style:** Single-line bold header combining Title, Company, and Dates; Italicized context line; Clean bullet spacing.

---

## 3. Template 03: Growth & Metrics (`03-growth-metrics`)
- **Best For:** Growth PMs, Performance Marketers, Data Engineers, and Monetization specialists.
- **Key Feature:** **Selected Impact Strip** located directly under the Professional Summary highlighting 3 top quantified career metrics.
- **Typography:** Clean sans-serif with bolded numbers and metric callout boxes.

---

## 4. Template 04: AI Product & Tech (`04-ai-product`)
- **Best For:** AI Product Managers, Machine Learning Engineers, Data Scientists, and LLM Specialists.
- **Key Feature:** Dedicated section for **AI & Technical Stack** (Models, Frameworks, Evaluation pipelines) and emphasis on tradeoffs (Latency, Cost, Accuracy).
- **Typography:** Modern technical sans-serif.

---

## 5. Template 05: Associate One-Pager (`05-associate-onepager`)
- **Best For:** APMs, Junior Engineers, Career Switchers, and Recent Graduates.
- **Key Feature:** High-density single page maximizing impact from academic projects, internships, leadership roles, and core technical skills.
- **Typography:** Tight leading, crisp sans-serif, optimized for maximum vertical space efficiency.
