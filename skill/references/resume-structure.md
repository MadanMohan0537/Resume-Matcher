# Resume Structure & Formatting Guide

This guide establishes the structural foundation required for any resume to pass both automated Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and the human 6-second scan.

---

## 1. Golden Layout Rules
1. **Single Column Only:** Never use multi-column or two-column layouts. ATS parsers read horizontally and will jumble dates, job titles, and text across columns.
2. **Standard Section Titles:** Only use standard headers that ATS parsers expect:
   - `Professional Summary` (or `Summary`)
   - `Core Competencies` (or `Core Skills`)
   - `Professional Experience` (or `Experience`)
   - `Education`
   - `Certifications`
3. **No Graphics, Tables, or Icons:** Avoid HTML tables for layout, photo headshots, or graphic skill bars.
4. **Length Benchmarks:**
   - **Under 5 years of total experience:** Strictly 1 page.
   - **5+ years of senior experience:** 2 full pages (page 2 must be at least 60% full; never leave an orphaned 3-line second page).

---

## 2. Recommended Section Order

1. **Header:** Candidate Name, Target Professional Title, Location (City, State / Remote), Contact (Email, Phone, LinkedIn, GitHub / Portfolio).
2. **Professional Summary:** 3 to 4 punchy sentences summarizing years of domain experience, key technical/product archetype, signature quantified win, and target position.
3. **Selected Impact Strip (Optional, for Growth/Metrics roles):** 3 standout quantified bullets.
4. **Core Competencies / Skills:** 8 to 16 keywords grouped logically.
5. **Professional Experience:**
   - Reverse chronological order.
   - Company name, Title, Location, and Dates (e.g., `Aug 2022 – Present`).
   - 1-line italicized company & domain context paragraph.
   - 4 to 7 outcome bullets for recent roles; 2 to 4 bullets for older roles.
6. **Education:** Degree, Major, Institution, Graduation Year.
7. **Certifications & Training:** Relevant industry credentials.
