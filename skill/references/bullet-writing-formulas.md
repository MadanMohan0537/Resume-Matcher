# Bullet Writing Formulas: Recruiter-Grade & ATS-Guaranteed

Recruiters spend **6 to 8 seconds** on their initial scan of a resume. Bullets formatted as passive duty lists ("Responsible for...") are immediately skipped. High-impact resumes lead with strong action verbs and quantified outcomes.

---

## 1. The Two Core Formulas

### Formula A: Google XYZ Formula
> **"Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]"**

- **[X] What did you accomplish?** (The core result)
- **[Y] How was it measured?** (The quantified metric or baseline comparison)
- **[Z] What did you actually do?** (The initiative, architecture, or workflow shipped)

*Example:*
> "Increased checkout conversion by 14% (adding ~$1.2M annual GMV) by redesigning the multi-step checkout into a one-tap flow with engineering and design."

---

### Formula B: PM & Tech TAR Formula
> **`[Action Verb] + [What you shipped or owned] + [For whom / Scope / Domain] + [Measurable Outcome]`**

*Example:*
> "Led migration of legacy payment monolith to distributed microservices across 4 squads, reducing p99 API latency from 450ms to 85ms and eliminating checkout downtime."

## 2. Before and After Transformations

| Weak (Duty / Process-Focused) | Strong (Outcome & Metric-Focused) |
|---|---|
| Responsible for user onboarding flow | Grew 30-day user activation from 22% to 38% by shipping an interactive onboarding checklist and A/B testing 4 drop-off points |
| Worked cross-functionally to build new AI features | Shipped enterprise LLM copilot serving 45,000 daily active users, improving response accuracy 28% while cutting token inference costs 40% |
| Managed product roadmap and backlog | Owned 18-month product roadmap across 3 engineering teams, prioritizing 12 major releases and cutting time-to-market 30% |
| Developed frontend web components in React | Re-architected core dashboard in React/TypeScript, cutting initial bundle size 65% and improving Google Lighthouse score from 54 to 96 |
| Maintained AWS cloud infrastructure | Automated CI/CD deployment pipelines on AWS/Kubernetes, reducing production deploy cycle time from 45 minutes to 4 minutes |

## 3. The 1-Line Company & Domain Context Rule

Always add ONE italicized context line under each position heading. Without context, a recruiter cannot judge the scale or stakes of your accomplishments.

**Pattern:**
> **Senior Product Manager / Senior Software Engineer** | Company Name | Dates  
> *[Company domain & business model] ([Scale, revenue, valuation, or user count]). [Your exact surface area and ownership].*

*Real Example:*
> **Lead Product Manager** | Lumofy | Aug 2024 – Present  
> *AI-powered talent-development SaaS ($40M valuation, 500+ MENA enterprises). Led 5 core product lines end-to-end and headed the product department.*

---

## 4. Power Action Verbs by Category

- **Leadership & Strategy:** Spearheaded, Architected, Steered, Championed, Founded, Orchestrated, Aligned, Directed.
- **Delivery & Execution:** Shipped, Deployed, Built, Launched, Engineered, Overhauled, Implemented, Streamlined.
- **Growth & Impact:** Accelerated, Scaled, Maximized, Grew, Expanded, Boosted, Generated, Outperformed.
- **Optimization & Efficiency:** Cut, Reduced, Eliminated, Automated, Consolidated, Restructured, Minimized, Replaced.
