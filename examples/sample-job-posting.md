# Target Job Posting: Lead Product Manager (Developer Platform & AI)
**Company:** ScaleGrid Technologies (Series B, $60M valuation)  
**Location:** Remote / San Francisco, CA  

### About the Role:
ScaleGrid is searching for a Lead Product Manager to own our next-generation Developer Platform & AI orchestration products. You will define product vision, align engineering squads, and scale our developer ecosystem from thousands to tens of thousands of active teams.

### Responsibilities:
- Lead the Developer Platform roadmap, balancing platform reliability, developer velocity, and AI capability expansion.
- Partner with engineering, architecture, and developer relations to deliver high-throughput, low-latency APIs.
- Drive product-led growth (PLG) initiatives, optimizing self-serve conversion, onboarding, and developer activation.
- Define and track metrics: API adoption, developer retention, Net Revenue Retention (NRR), and time-to-market.
- Conduct continuous user research with engineering leaders, architects, and open-source contributors.

### Qualifications:
- 5+ years of product management experience building developer tools, cloud infrastructure, or AI platforms.
- Strong technical fluency: understanding of APIs, distributed systems, streaming architectures, and cloud deployments.
- Demonstrated track record driving quantified business outcomes ($ ARR growth, conversion lift, latency reduction).
- Experience leading cross-functional teams using OKRs and outcome-focused roadmaps.
